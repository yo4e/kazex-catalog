## English

Heijitsu (平日), the debut album by ThaMall品川, takes its inspiration from an unexpectedly vivid scene: dry, looping music drifting endlessly from a rusted loudspeaker in a spacious local park. That ordinary moment held a peculiar blend of happiness and emptiness, as if everyday life had slipped into a dream. Drawing on mallsoft, the album invites listeners into that in-between space: familiar, quietly comforting, yet strangely vacant. Its starting point is a small, easily overlooked experience, transformed into an invitation to hear the everyday differently.
